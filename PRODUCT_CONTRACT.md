# Ruwaq — Product Contract v1

> **Vision:** Easiest and most trusted proposal partner for every segment.  
> **Focus now:** Saudi real estate sector only.

---

## What Ruwaq Is

Ruwaq translates what you know about a project into a professional proposal document that builds client trust.

**Not:** PDF generator · form builder · template library · Proposify clone.

---

## The 3 Anchors (User Input)

| Anchor | Required |
|--------|----------|
| Client | Yes |
| Work description | Yes |
| Commercial intent | **Fixed price** OR **Estimate only** |

Everything else comes from: company profile (once) · AI (organize) · templates · human review.

---

## Universal Document (11 Layers)

1. Company identity  
2. Proposal metadata (client, date, ref, validity)  
3. Project understanding  
4. Scope of work  
5. Deliverables  
6. Commercial offer (+ VAT when applicable)  
7. Payment terms  
8. Timeline  
9. Assumptions  
10. Exclusions  
11. Validity & acceptance  

Real estate depth: property type, area, phases (design → execution → handover), site access, permits exclusions.

---

## AI Rules

**May infer:** scope structure, deliverables, assumptions, exclusions, rough timeline, introduction tone.

**Must never invent:** final price, VAT status, guarantees, legal commitments, certifications, binding dates, client legal names.

---

## Input Tiers

| Tier | Fields |
|------|--------|
| **0 Required** | Client · work description · price or estimate |
| **1 Optional (collapsed)** | Location · property type · area · duration · specs |
| **2 Later** | Paste WhatsApp · file upload |

Optional fields enrich AI output — never block generation.

## Review Gates (Product — Not Optional UX)

Before export, user must confirm:

1. **Assumptions**  
2. **Exclusions**  
3. **Commercial terms** (price or estimate label)

---

## Commercial Modes

| Mode | Behavior |
|------|----------|
| `fixed_price` | User provides SAR amount; AI builds payment schedule |
| `estimate_only` | Document labeled preliminary; confirmed after site visit |

---

## Success Criteria

- Intent → sendable document in **< 10 minutes**
- User input **≤ 5 minutes**
- Client reaction: *"This looks like an established office"*
- Assumptions + exclusions visible in every export

---

## Deferred (Intentionally)

E-sign · online payments · full BOQ · CRM · WhatsApp paste · government RFPs.

**Order:** excellent document → context capture → close the deal.
